# Contributing to AilaFlow

Thank you for your interest in contributing to AilaFlow.

AilaFlow is distributed under the [MIT License](./LICENSE.md). By submitting a contribution, you agree that it may be distributed under the same license.

## How to Contribute

1. Fork the repository.
2. Create a focused branch for your change.
3. Make your changes and add or update tests where appropriate.
4. Make sure the existing tests and checks pass.
5. Open a pull request with a clear explanation of what changed and why.

Keep contributions focused, avoid unrelated refactoring, and identify the source and license of any third-party material you introduce.
